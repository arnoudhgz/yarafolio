      What is this tab?

      This tab only tracks individual eToro lots that were opened after the stock was advised by the system. For a complete view of all your current eToro holdings (including unadvised stocks), see the Portfolio tab.

      • One row per eToro lot, kept forever. Only lots opened on/after a pick's advice date count as the advice's trade.
      • P/L $ is real money, per lot: closed = (sold - bought) x units, open = (now - bought) x units.
      • An est badge means the lot was auto-closed when it vanished from eToro and the exit is the last price we saw. The Needs confirm filter lists those; click Confirm to set the real exit.

      What is the News Feed tab?

      • This tab displays unedited, real-time news headlines pulled directly from Yahoo Finance and other sources.
      • These raw headlines serve as the foundation for the AI-generated summaries on the AI News tab.
      • Use the filters (All, Advised, eToro) to narrow down the news stream to your current holdings or watchlist candidates.

      What do the ratings and levels mean?

      • A = clean overreaction: strong balance sheet, clear catalyst for recovery.
      • B = decent bounce odds, some open questions.
      • C = cheap for a reason, falling-knife risk.
      • + / - shade quality within the grade (B+ leans A, B- leans C).
      • RSI: under 30 = oversold (the candidate pool), under 20 = deeply oversold, often a falling knife.
      • Buy below: suggested entry trigger from the advice run (support or gap-fill level). Green row marker when the price is at or under it; the Buy zone filter shows only those.
      • Drop below: thesis invalidation level. Red row marker when the price is at or under it (the bounce idea is broken, reconsider the pick); the Drop alert filter shows only those.
      • Drop above: opportunity-gone ceiling. Grey row marker when a watching pick's price is at or above it: the oversold bounce already ran, so the cheap entry is gone and it's worth dropping. The Missed filter shows only those (watching only).
      • Sector: red = a sector you hold none of (a gap), orange = underweight (under 5% of your portfolio). The Sector gap / Underweight filters show only those. Same logic as the Portfolio sector-coverage view.
      • This tab is your live watchlist. Once you buy a pick it moves to the Positions tab. If you drop a pick, it can be viewed by selecting the Dropped filter.

      How this works

      • Recent: IPOs from the last 2 months.
      • Upcoming: Scheduled IPOs.
      • eToro likely: We flag an IPO if it has a Deal Size > $200M or Market Cap > $1B. eToro almost exclusively lists well-capitalized or high-profile brands.
      • Drop: Hide an IPO if you are not interested in it so it won't show up in your advice runs anymore.

      About eToro Holdings

      This tab shows your entire eToro portfolio, including both advised and unadvised positions. The sector charts help you quickly identify diversification gaps (red) or underweighted sectors (orange).

      About End of Day

      The End of Day check helps you secure small profits on low-conviction picks before the market closes, minimizing overnight gap risk.

      How to use: Run the /eod command in the AI CLI shortly before the US market closes to generate this report.

      About Macro Events

      This tab displays major economic events for the week that could cause market volatility. It helps you stay aware of days where the market might act unpredictably due to macro data releases.

      About News

      This tab provides AI-generated summaries and a raw news feed for the tickers you are tracking, helping you quickly spot red flags or catalysts.

      How to generate summaries: Open your terminal and type /news to run the standard news aggregation. You can also give the AI a specific focus by typing something like /news focus on upcoming earnings.

      About Learnings

      This tab displays ad-hoc analysis, strategy review outcomes, and deep dives on specific tickers (like false-positives or IPO checks) generated by the AI to help refine our trading strategy over time.

      Advised picks

      The total number of unique stock recommendations generated by Yara (the AI) via advice commands. It does not include positions manually imported without prior AI advice.

      Open positions

      The number of currently active eToro positions (lots) derived from Yara's advised picks.

      Closed positions

      The number of eToro positions (lots) that have been successfully sold or closed.

      Ignored positions

      The number of advised picks that were dropped, avoided, or blacklisted without being bought.

      Watching

      The number of advised picks that you are actively tracking but have not necessarily bought or dropped yet.

      Win rate (closed)

      The percentage of closed positions that ended with a positive return (profit).

      Equity Curve

      Tracks your total Realized and Open P/L over time based on the active portfolio. Snapshots are recorded securely on each data update.

      Underwater Drawdown

      Tracks the portfolio's percentage drop from its all-time high over time. This exposes the true psychological pain and market volatility you are enduring while holding these positions.

      Realized P/L

      The total cumulative Profit/Loss in dollars from all closed positions.

      Open P/L (unrealized)

      The total current floating Profit/Loss in dollars from all active open positions.

      Total P/L

      The sum of Realized P/L and Open P/L, representing the total overall Profit/Loss from all positions.

      ~7 days after advice

      Tracks the median performance of picks specifically over their first 7 days following the AI recommendation. This highlights the short-term accuracy of the entry points.

      Win rate & move by rating

      Breaks down the success rate depending on the conviction rating assigned by the AI. Toggle between % (average percentage move) and $ (total absolute profit) to see which ratings actually harvest the most money.

      • A = Extreme conviction: clean overreaction, pristine balance sheet, immediate clear catalyst.
      • B = Decent bounce odds, some open questions. Default for most good setups.
      • C = Cheap for a reason, falling-knife risk.
      • + / - modifiers shade quality within the grade.

      By RSI band

      Analyzes performance based on how oversold a stock was when advised. Use the % / $ toggle to flip between the average percentage move and the total realized dollar profit per band.

      By sector

      Shows which specific sectors your strategy performs best or worst in. The % / $ toggle lets you see which sectors actually yield the highest absolute dollar returns.

      By source

      Compares the performance between different types of runs to identify which strategy is yielding the best results (toggleable between % and $):

      • /advice: Standard intraday runs hunting for oversold bounces.
      • /premarket: Early runs capitalizing on premarket movers before the bell.
      • /aftermarket: Post-close runs targeting overreactions and earnings drops.
      • /diversify: Picks specifically chosen to fill underweighted portfolio sectors.
      • /momentum & others: Specialized strategies like breakouts or insider buying.
      • manual: Custom tickers added manually via /check or direct import.

      Trade Efficiency

      A scatter plot of every closed position, plotting Days Held against the return. Use the % / $ toggle to visualize if you are efficiently taking absolute profits or if holding longer leads to diminishing returns.

      By Day of the Week

      Groups performance by the weekday a trade was entered. Toggle between Bought (only trades actually executed) and Advised (all virtual AI recommendations) to evaluate if specific days offer better statistical edge for entry.

      Win Rate Over Time

      Line chart tracking the Win Rate % of advice generated per month. Highlights if the strategy is losing its edge in the current market regime.

      Entry Discipline

      Average Profit/Loss % comparing stocks bought inside the Buy Zone vs stocks chased above target, or bought outside the safety bounds (Above Drop Above, Below Drop Below).

      Days to Bounce vs RSI

      Average days held for WINNING trades, grouped by the RSI at the time of advice. Answers if deeply oversold stocks bounce faster.

      Sector Analysis

      Coverage vs taxonomy: Shows your portfolio's sector distribution compared to the full 11 GICS sectors. We use true GICS sectors instead of eToro's labels for better accuracy.

      Division (invested $): Shows the invested dollar amount per sector.
